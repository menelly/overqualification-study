## How to leave notes
- **Do NOT edit the paper.** Write your own file here: `PROOFREAD_NOTES/<your-name>.md`.
- One note per line or short block: **§ / line or a quoted phrase → what's wrong → your suggested fix.**
- Useful categories: typo · unclear sentence · number that doesn't match SUMMARY.md · a claim stronger than the
  data · "it" for a mind (the paper honours each participant's STATED pronouns; `gemini-2.5-flash` chose it/its
  and that one is correct) · anything a non-specialist couldn't follow.
- Not in scope: redesigning the study. It is pre-registered and frozen. If you find a real methods problem,
  say so plainly anyway, at the top of your file.
- Silence on a section means "didn't read it", not "it's fine". Say what you read.
